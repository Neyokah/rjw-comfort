Coverage
- All production Harmony patch targets install against the actual RJW assembly.
- Adult eligibility boundaries, invalid numeric settings and resource arithmetic.
- 10,000 randomized partial-placement accounting cases, callback clamping,
  fractional balance retention and placement followed by exception.
- Exact queued/current native job matching, queue removal and queue timeout.
- Native job lifecycle callback attachment and interrupted-driver cleanup.
- Completion scope, repeated callbacks, exceptions, unrelated nested events and
  another patch skipping the original method.
- Actual Scribe save/load round-trip, legacy-format migration, loaded-driver
  rebinding and protection against an old callback canceling a newer session.
- Optional Cumpilation definition lookup, nondefault conversion ratios, standalone
  fallback and the distinction between measured zero and unavailable measurement.
- Lazy map-cache interval, population changes and clock rewind invalidation.
- Compiled extraction callback wiring and absence of population searches from
  the extraction job's per-tick failure check.

Search benchmark
Controlled no-target fixture: 100 workers, 600 ticks, 500 pawns.
Original 1.0 DLL: 30,000,000 eligibility checks.
Rewritten implementation: 2,500 eligibility checks.
Observed timings are recorded in test-results.txt. Low-level eligibility is
stubbed equally for both implementations. This measures isolated search cost,
not full eligibility cost, overall frame time or colony TPS.

Limits
The suite exercises real game/RJW types and installed Harmony targets using
controlled fixtures. The native completion body is replaced by a fixture while
testing Comfort's production hooks; this is not a simulated full colony.
Resource tests include controlled placement callbacks and compiled-call checks;
they do not exercise every real map/storage layout. No live colony soak test,
full installed-mod interaction test, multiplayer sync test or TPS measurement
was performed. Gameplay validation remains necessary for those claims.
